## Question

How should a small screen communicate relationships between entries without forcing the reader to pan around a diagram?

## Hypothesis

A compact linked list can preserve the target ID, title, collection, and relationship label. Those are the pieces needed to understand the connection and navigate to it.

## Observation

The supplied Blueprint Console reference gives the desktop homepage a schematic node map. Its mobile layout places navigation, the introduction, and content previews first. The article layout uses disclosures for metadata and linked entries.

## Initial approach

Use the schematic as a desktop overview and show the same real relationships as a compact list on mobile. Keep article contents and linked nodes in accessible disclosures on narrow screens.

## Next step

Evaluate the representation with real entries and user feedback. The current implementation establishes the interaction, but no usability outcome is claimed here.
